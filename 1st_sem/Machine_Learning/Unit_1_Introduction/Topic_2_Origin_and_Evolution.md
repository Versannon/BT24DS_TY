# Topic 2: Origin and Evolution of Machine Learning

## Historical Milestones
1. **Early Foundations (1940s-1950s)**: Walter Pitts and Warren McCulloch introduced the first mathematical model of a neuron. Alan Turing proposed the "Turing Test" and the concept of learning machines.
2. **The Perceptron (1957)**: Frank Rosenblatt created the Perceptron, a hardware-based single-layer binary classifier.
3. **The First AI Winter (1970s)**: Minsky and Papert proved that the single-layer perceptron could not solve non-linear problems like XOR, leading to a major drop in funding.
4. **Developments in Statistical Learning Theory (1980s-1990s)**: Backpropagation was popularized, enabling multi-layer neural network training. Support Vector Machines (SVMs) were introduced by Vladimir Vapnik, offering strong mathematical guarantees.
5. **Rise of Deep Learning (2010s-Present)**: Massive datasets (ImageNet) and high-compute GPUs enabled training deep networks (AlexNet, ResNet), leading to the contemporary era of Generative AI.
