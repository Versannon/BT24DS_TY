# Topic 3: Descriptive Statistics and Correlation

- **Measures of Central Tendency**: Mean, Median, Mode.
- **Measures of Dispersion**: Range, Variance, Standard Deviation, IQR.
- **Measures of Shape**: Skewness (asymmetry), Kurtosis (peakedness).
- **Correlation**: Pearson ($r$, linear), Spearman (monotonic ranks), Kendall's Tau. *Correlation does not imply causation.*
