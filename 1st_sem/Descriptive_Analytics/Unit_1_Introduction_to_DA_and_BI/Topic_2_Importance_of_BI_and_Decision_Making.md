# Topic 2: Business Intelligence and Decision-Making

## Business Intelligence (BI)
BI is the set of strategies, processes, and technologies that transform raw data into actionable insights for decision-making.

## Decision Support Levels
- **Strategic Decisions**: Long-term directional decisions (e.g., merging with a company).
- **Tactical Decisions**: Medium-term operations (e.g., hiring 5 new engineers).
- **Operational Decisions**: Daily immediate choices (e.g., approving a customer refund).
