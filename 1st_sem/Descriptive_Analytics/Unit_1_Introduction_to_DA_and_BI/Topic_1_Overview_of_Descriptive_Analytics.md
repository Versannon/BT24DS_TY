# Topic 1: Overview of Descriptive Analytics

Descriptive Analytics is the baseline stage of analytics that focuses on summarizing historical data to answer: *"What happened?"*

## Key Concepts
- **Metric**: A standard of measurement (e.g. page views).
- **Measure**: Concrete values associated with a metric (e.g. 5,000 views).
- **Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**: A metric that measures progress toward strategic objectives (e.g. customer retention rate).
