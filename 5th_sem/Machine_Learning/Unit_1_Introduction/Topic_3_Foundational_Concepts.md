# Topic 3: Foundational Concepts in Machine Learning

## Abstraction
Abstraction is the process of translating real-world phenomena into numerical vectors. For example, representing a text document as a bag-of-words vector, or an image as a matrix of RGB pixel intensities.

## Knowledge Representation
How a model represents its learned patterns:
- **Decision Trees**: Logical rules arranged hierarchically.
- **Support Vector Machines**: Hyperplanes separating data points in a high-dimensional space.
- **Neural Networks**: Large matrices of floating-point weights and activation functions.

## Generalization
The goal of machine learning is to perform accurately on new, unseen data, not just the training set.
- **Overfitting**: Model memorizes noise in the training data (high variance, low bias). Performs poorly on test data.
- **Underfitting**: Model is too simple to capture the underlying pattern (low variance, high bias). Performs poorly on both training and test data.
