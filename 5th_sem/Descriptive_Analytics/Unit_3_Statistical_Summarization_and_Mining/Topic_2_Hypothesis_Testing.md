# Topic 2: Hypothesis Testing and Data Mining Process

## Hypothesis Testing
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$)**: No effect.
- **Alternative ($H_1$)**: Significant effect.
- **P-value**: Probability of observed results assuming $H_0$ is true. If $p < 0.05$, reject $H_0$.
- *Tests*: T-Test (one-sample, independent two-sample, paired), Z-Test, ANOVA, Chi-Square.

## Data Mining Methodologies
- CRISP-DM, SEMMA, KDD.
