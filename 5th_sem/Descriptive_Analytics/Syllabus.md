# BTDS-302-PCC: DESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS SYLLABUS

## Course Objectives
1. Understand the role and importance of descriptive analytics and business intelligence (BI) in decision-making.
2. Familiarize students with BI architecture and decision support systems (DSS).
3. Learn methods for data collection, cleaning, and preparation for analysis.
4. Develop skills in exploratory data analysis (EDA) and data visualization.
5. Learn to design reports, dashboards, and scorecards for data visualization.
6. Apply statistical techniques and data mining approaches to summarize and interpret data.
7. Build and manage BI projects effectively.

## Course Outcomes
After successful completion of the course, students will be able to:
- Explain principles of descriptive analytics and BI.
- Describe components of BI architecture and DSS.
- Perform data collection, cleaning, and preparation (imputing, outlier handling).
- Compute descriptive statistics and perform EDA.
- Conduct hypothesis testing and apply association rule mining/clustering.
- Build dashboards using tools like Excel, Power BI, and Tableau.
- Apply storytelling techniques and address ethical issues in data handling.

## Unit-wise Syllabus

### UNIT-I: Introduction to Descriptive Analytics & BI, Architecture and DSS (07 Hours)
- **Overview of Descriptive Analytics**: Definition, scope, role in analytics spectrum. Key concepts: metrics, measures, KPIs. Historical development. Tools overview.
- **Importance of BI**: Definition, scope, components, benefits, applications in healthcare, retail, finance. Integration of descriptive analytics in BI.
- **Role in Decision-Making**: Strategic, tactical, and operational decisions. Rational decision-making model. Case studies.
- **BI Architecture**: Data integration (ETL), data warehousing (DWH), star schema vs. snowflake schema, OLAP operations (slice, dice, drill down, roll up), MOLAP/ROLAP/HOLAP. Data lakes vs. DWHs.
- **Decision Support Systems (DSS)**: DBMS, model-based system, user interface. Types: data-driven, model-driven, knowledge-driven. What-if and sensitivity analysis.
- **BI Project Lifecycle**: Planning, requirements gathering, design, implementation, testing, deployment, maintenance, ROI measurement.

### UNIT-II: Data Collection & Preparation, Exploratory Data Analysis (08 Hours)
- **Data Sources**: Structured (databases), unstructured (text, images, videos), semi-structured (XML, JSON). Internal (CRM, ERP) vs. external data.
- **Collection Methods**: Manual vs. automated (web scraping, APIs), real-time collection (IoT). Legal, compliance, and ethical issues.
- **Data Cleaning**: Typos, inconsistencies, duplicates, normalization/standardization, aggregation.
- **Missing Data & Outliers**: MCAR, MAR, MNAR. Deletion vs. imputation (mean, median, regression, KNN). Outlier detection (Z-score, IQR) and treatment (capping, removal).
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Objectives (uncovering patterns, spotting anomalies, testing assumptions), initial inspection.
- **Descriptive Statistics**: Central tendency (mean, median, mode) and dispersion (range, variance, standard deviation, IQR). Shape measures (skewness, kurtosis). Visualizations (histograms, box plots, bar charts).
- **Patterns & Trends**: Time series concepts (trends, seasonality, cycles). Pattern recognition (clustering, classification). Anomaly detection.
- **Correlation**: Pearson, Spearman, Kendall Tau. Interpretation (strength, direction, correlation vs. causation). Visualizations (heatmaps, scatter plots).

### UNIT-III: Statistical Methods & Advanced Data Mining Techniques (08 Hours)
- **Data Summarization**: Central tendency and dispersion comparisons (impact of outliers, when to use which measure).
- **Probability Distributions**: Discrete (Binomial, Poisson) and continuous (Normal, Exponential). Fitting data to distributions.
- **Hypothesis Testing**: Formulation, steps, p-values, confidence intervals. Parametric vs. non-parametric. Z-test, T-test (one-sample, two-sample), Chi-square test, ANOVA.
- **Data Mining Process**: Methodologies (CRISP-DM, SEMMA, KDD). Data mining vs. machine learning.
- **Clustering & Classification**: K-Means, Hierarchical, DBSCAN. Decision trees (Gini, Entropy, pruning), Random Forest, SVM, Naive Bayes.
- **Association Rule Mining**: Support, confidence, lift. Apriori algorithm, Eclat, FP-growth.
- **Anomaly Detection**: Statistical methods vs. ML-based (Isolation Forest, One-class SVM, Autoencoders).

### UNIT-IV: Data Visualization and BI Tools (07 Hours)
- **Principles of Visualization**: Clarity, simplicity, accuracy, aesthetics. Visual encoding. Accessibility.
- **Charts and Graphs**: Bar, line, pie (alternatives), scatter, bubble, heatmaps. Best practices.
- **Dashboards & Scorecards**: Strategic, tactical, and operational dashboards. Visual hierarchy. Scorecards and KPIs.
- **Data Storytelling**: Narrative arc (intro, rising action, climax, conclusion). Combining text and visuals.
- **BI Tools**: Excel (PivotTables, Power Query, Power Pivot), Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn), R (dplyr, ggplot2, Shiny), Power BI, Tableau.

### UNIT-V: Building & Managing BI Projects, Real-World Applications (08 Hours)
- **BI Project Management**: Goal setting, timelines, resource allocation, risk management. Agile practices in BI (sprints, backlogs).
- **Developing Reports & Dashboards**: Operational vs. strategic reporting. KPI metrics selection.
- **Real-World Case Studies**: Retail sales dashboard, Customer insights project. Lessons learned and best practices.
- **Measuring BI Success**: Frameworks, KPIs, ROI calculations, quantitative and qualitative measures, continuous feedback loop.
- **Industry Applications**: Retail (demand forecasting, basket analysis), Healthcare (patient outcomes, resources), Finance (fraud, risk assessment), Manufacturing (predictive maintenance).

### UNIT-VI: Communication, Storytelling, Ethics & Best Practices in BI (07 Hours)
- **Effective Storytelling**: Crafting narrative with data, simplifying complexity, metaphors.
- **Compelling Reports & Presentations**: Writing structure, slide design principles (balance, animations), tailoring to audience.
- **Stakeholder Presentation**: Public speaking tips, voice modulation, managing nervousness, handling difficult Q&A.
- **Ethics in BI**: Ethical data collection, usage, and manipulation.
- **Data Privacy & Security**: GDPR, HIPAA basics, data governance.
- **Sustainable BI**: Green computing in data centers, sustainable data governance.
