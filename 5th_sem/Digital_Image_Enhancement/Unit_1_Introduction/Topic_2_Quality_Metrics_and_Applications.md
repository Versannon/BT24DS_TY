# Topic 2: Quality Metrics and Applications

- **Mean Squared Error (MSE)**:
  $$MSE = \frac{1}{m \times n} \sum [I(i, j) - K(i, j)]^2$$
- **Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR)**:
  $$PSNR = 10 \cdot \log_{10} \left( \frac{MAX_I^2}{MSE} \right)$$
- **Applications**: Medicine (CT/MRI contrast), Remote Sensing (haze removal), Surveillance (license plates).
