# Topic 2: Canny Edge Detector

Canny edge detector stages:
1. **Gaussian Blur**: Noise removal.
2. **Gradient Estimation**: Compute magnitudes/directions.
3. **Non-Maximum Suppression**: Thicken edges.
4. **Double Thresholding**: Filter weak and strong edges.
5. **Hysteresis Edge Tracking**: Finalize weak edges connected to strong ones.
